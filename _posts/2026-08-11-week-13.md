This week I developed the rule-based symbolic component.

Unlike the neural model, which uses the surrounding natural-language context, the symbolic system operates on the structured **Action, Object, and Modifier** annotations.

For each malware capability, I defined sets of behavioral indicators. For example, anti-detection included actions such as *hide*, *evade*, *disable*, and *obfuscate*, while data exfiltration included *send*, *upload*, *transfer*, and *exfiltrate*.

Rules also considered contextual objects and modifiers. A match on an action contributed two points, while a contextual match contributed another two points. If both types of evidence were present, the rule received an additional one-point bonus.

The final score was normalized between zero and one.

I intentionally kept the symbolic system relatively simple and interpretable so that I could examine exactly why it made particular predictions.
