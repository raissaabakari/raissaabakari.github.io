title: "Week 10: Designing the Data Split"
---

This week focused on designing the evaluation procedure.

One concern was potential information leakage. If events were randomly split into training and test sets, different sentences or events from the same threat report could appear in both partitions. A model could then benefit from report-specific vocabulary rather than truly generalizing to unseen documents.

To avoid this, I split the data by **complete threat report**.

The final partition contained:

- 1,150 events from 24 reports in the development subset;
- 348 events from seven reports in the validation subset;
- 298 events from eight reports in the held-out test subset.

This structure kept every report entirely within a single partition.

Although the project contains a development partition, the final neural model was not fine-tuned on these examples. The validation set was used later to select the neural-symbolic fusion weight, while the test set was reserved for final evaluation.
