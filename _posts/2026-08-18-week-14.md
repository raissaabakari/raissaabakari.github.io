This week's work highlighted one of the central challenges of the symbolic approach: **malware behaviors overlap**.

Many seemingly useful indicators are not unique to a single capability. For example:

- a remote server may be involved in command and control or data exfiltration;
- HTTP may be used for multiple forms of network communication;
- installing software can be related to infection as well as establishing machine access;
- sending data does not necessarily imply exfiltration.

This meant the symbolic rules sometimes identified a relevant security concept but assigned it to the wrong behavioral category.

Rather than eliminating these errors entirely, I decided they were an important part of the research question. A major reason to build a hybrid system was to investigate whether the surrounding neural context could compensate for ambiguity in explicit symbolic rules.
