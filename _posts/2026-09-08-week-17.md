title: "Week 17: Final Evaluation"
---

This week I ran the final evaluation on the held-out test subset of 298 malware-behavior events.

The three systems produced the following results:

- **Neural baseline:** 46.3% accuracy, 0.421 macro-F1
- **Symbolic baseline:** 52.7% accuracy, 0.555 macro-F1
- **Hybrid model:** 56.4% accuracy, 0.553 macro-F1

The hybrid model produced the highest overall accuracy, improving approximately ten percentage points over the neural semantic baseline.

However, the result was more complicated than simply saying that the hybrid model was “best.” The symbolic baseline actually achieved a slightly higher macro-F1 than the hybrid model.

This suggested that much of the benefit came from the structured malware information itself and reinforced the importance of including both neural-only and symbolic-only baselines.
