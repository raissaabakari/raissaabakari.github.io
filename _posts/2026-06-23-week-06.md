---

This week's main task was finding a dataset that could support the revised research question.

The ideal dataset needed two things. First, it needed natural-language descriptions of malware behavior so that I could apply neural semantic representations. Second, it needed some form of structured behavioral information that could support a symbolic component.

I explored several possible cybersecurity resources and considered whether frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK could be incorporated.

Eventually, I identified **MalwareTextDB**, a corpus of annotated Advanced Persistent Threat reports. MalwareTextDB was especially promising because it includes both original threat-report language and structured annotations representing actions, subjects, objects, modifiers, malware capabilities, and tactical and strategic objectives.

This gave me exactly the two representations needed for the experiment: unstructured text for the neural component and structured relationships for the symbolic component.
