This week focused on interpreting the experiment and preparing the final technical paper.

One of the most important conclusions was that the results should not be interpreted as evidence that neuro-symbolic systems automatically outperform either neural or symbolic methods.

The hybrid system achieved the highest accuracy, but the symbolic baseline achieved a marginally higher macro-F1. This indicates that explicit domain knowledge itself was particularly informative for the task.

I also documented several limitations, including the age and small size of MalwareTextDB, the use of only six capability classes, manually constructed symbolic rules, a general-purpose rather than cybersecurity-specific sentence encoder, and evaluation on a single report-level split.

Potential future directions include richer symbolic knowledge graphs, adaptive neural-symbolic weighting, newer threat-intelligence corpora, domain-specific neural models, and multi-label classification.
