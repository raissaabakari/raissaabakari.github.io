---

This week I implemented the actual neuro-symbolic component.

For each event and candidate malware capability, the system now had two scores:

1. a neural semantic-similarity score;
2. a normalized symbolic rule score.

The hybrid model combines these using a weighted sum.

A weight closer to 1 places more emphasis on the neural representation, while a lower value gives the symbolic component more influence.

I tested neural weights ranging from 0.0 to 1.0 in increments of 0.1 on the validation set.

Because the target classes were imbalanced, I selected the final weight primarily using **macro-F1** rather than accuracy. This ensured that smaller malware capability classes influenced the selection process rather than allowing the largest categories to dominate.
