This week I analyzed the validation results and selected the final hybrid configuration.

The best validation macro-F1 occurred at a neural weight of:

**α = 0.8**

This meant the final hybrid classifier used an **80% neural contribution and 20% symbolic contribution**.

Interestingly, the configuration with the highest validation accuracy was not exactly the same configuration that achieved the highest macro-F1. Since macro-F1 better reflected balanced performance across the six unevenly represented classes, I retained the 0.8 setting.

Once the weighting parameter had been chosen, I froze the experimental configuration before evaluating the held-out test data.

This was important methodologically because I did not want to adjust the system based on the final test results.
