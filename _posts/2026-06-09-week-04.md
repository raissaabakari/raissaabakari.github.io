This week I focused more heavily on **neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence** and retrieval-augmented systems.

One of the major papers guiding the early project was **SymRAG**, which uses symbolic, neural, and hybrid processing pathways and dynamically selects among them depending on the query. I was particularly interested in the broader idea behind the system: neural and symbolic approaches do not necessarily need to compete. They may capture different forms of information and can potentially complement one another.

I also read broader neuro-symbolic AI research and work involving retrieval-augmented generation. At first, I considered whether the project itself might involve a RAG architecture.

As the literature review progressed, however, it became clear that reproducing a system such as SymRAG would be too large for the scope of this project. Instead, I began thinking about a smaller experiment that would preserve the central question: **what happens when neural and symbolic evidence are combined for a cybersecurity task?**
